## What each package includes

| | Video API | Feed + Creator Studio | Data Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data in | Your snapshots via `from-data`, authored scripts | Your listing, trade and wallet data | We bind a feed (DefiLlama, CoinGecko, Nansen, your API) |
| Formats | Any authored script, show kits on your channel | Kits including user-facing ones (Bag Check, Roast) | Two or more kits designed and locked for you |
| Brand | Per call or locked on your channel | Locked on your channel | Locked, with compliance language |
| Users creating | Optional embed | Embedded studio, user credits, claim flow, review queue | No |
| Delivery | Keyed URLs, webhooks, share pages | In-app feed, share pages with CTA, autopost | Webhook, Drive, Buffer autopost |
| Readout | Usage and cost | Exposed users vs comparison group: views, creation rate, retention, activity | Videos delivered, cost per video |
